⚔️ Server Guide: RAIDS!

Welcome to the new era of warfare on the network! We have officially implemented a custom Dynamic Raid System integrated directly with KingdomsX. This system replaces unpredictable griefing and offline raiding with structured, balanced, and strategic warfare. If you want to claim dominance or pillage your rivals, you must do it face-to-face. Here is everything you need to know to lead your nation to victory or defend your homeland.

📜 How to Initiate a Raid

Only the King of a nation has the authority to declare a raid. To start a war declaration, use the following commands:

/raid <nation_name> aggression
/raid <nation_name> ransack

The Golden Rules of Engagement

  • 1. The 10% Rule: You cannot offline-raid. A raid can only be declared if at least 10% of the defending nation's members are currently online.
  • 2. Peaceful Exemption: If a nation has chosen a peaceful doctrine, they are entirely immune. Peaceful nations cannot declare raids, and you cannot declare raids on them.

🛡️ The 15-Minute Preparation Phase

  • 🔹 The defending nation will receive a clean, non-intrusive broadcast alert in chat.
  • 🔹 A custom BossBar will appear at the top of the screen for all defending members, starting a 15-minute countdown timer.
  • 🔹 Use this time wisely! Gather your allies, log into Discord, grab your gear, and fortify your positions before the countdown hits zero.

🏹 The Two Raid Types (Duration: 1 Hour)

Once the 15-minute preparation timer ends, the BossBar will transition into a 1-hour countdown. Depending on what the attacking King chose, the rules of engagement will strictly change:

1. Aggression Raids
  • 🎯 The Objective: Pure territorial combat and military dominance.
  • ✅ What you CAN do: Engage in full PvP and kill enemy players anywhere within their own territory.
  • ❌ What you CANNOT do: This is strictly a battle of blades. Attackers cannot break blocks, open doors, loot chests, or interact with any builds/containers.
  • ⏱️ Cooldown: Once finished, the attacking nation faces a 3-day (72-hour) cooldown before they can declare this raid type again.
2. Ransack Raids
  • 🎯 The Objective: High-stakes heist and economic theft.
  • ✅ What you CAN do: Infiltrate enemy territory to open and loot containers, chests, and storage bins to steal their valuables.
  • ❌ What you CANNOT do: This is a robbery, not a massacre. PvP is completely disabled, you cannot kill players during a Ransack raid. Additionally, you cannot break blocks or open mechanical builds/doors. You must find your way to the loot naturally.
  • ⏱️ Cooldown: Once finished, the attacking nation faces a 3-day (72-hour) cooldown before they can declare this raid type again.
⚠️ Important Note: Attempting to bypass these mechanics or exploiting limitations during the transition phases will be met with immediate administrative action. Guard your vaults, ready your blades, and may the best nation win!

✈️ Drones — Player Guide

Launch strike drones at target coordinates, deploy interceptors, and craft upgrades.

Getting Started

You need two things to strike a target:

  • 1. A drone item in your hand (craft one or ask an admin for one)
  • 2. The coordinates of your target (F3 shows your XYZ position)

The Four Drone Tiers

Tier Name Explosion Sets Fire Speed
I §7 Tier-I Strike Drone Small ✗ Slow
II §a Tier-II Strike Drone Medium ✗ Normal
III §9 Tier-III Strike Drone Large ✔ Fast
IV §4 Tier-IV Strike Drone Massive ✔ Fastest

Each tier produces a bigger multi-phase explosion effect with rising smoke columns that linger at the impact site.

How to Launch a Drone

Step 1 — Hold the drone: Put the drone item in your main hand (your right hand slot).

Step 2 — Pick your target: Use F3 to find the XYZ coordinates of where you want it to hit, or ask someone to stand there and read their coords.

Step 3 — Fire:

/dronetarget <x> <y> <z>

Examples:

/dronetarget 100 64 -250
/dronetarget ~50 ~ ~0
💡 Tip: You can use `~` for relative coordinates. `~50` means 50 blocks from where you are standing. `~ ~ ~` would hit your own feet — don't do that.

Crafting Drones

Open the crafting GUI with:

/dronecraftgui
[ Ingredient 1 ] [ Ingredient 2 ] [ Ingredient 3 ] [ Ingredient 4 ] → [ DRONE (click!) ]

To craft:

  • 1. Open /dronecraftgui
  • 2. Make sure you have the required materials in your inventory
  • 3. Click the drone icon on the right side of the recipe row
  • 4. The materials are consumed and the drone is added to your inventory automatically
  • 5. The GUI refreshes instantly so you can craft again
⚠️ Inventory full? The crafted item will drop at your feet.

Drone Interceptors

An Interceptor is a placeable defense turret that automatically destroys any incoming drone that flies within its detection radius.

Getting an interceptor: Craft one via /dronecraftgui (last row)

Deploying an interceptor: Hold the item in your main hand and right-click the top face of a block where you want to place it.

Rule Detail
Single use Each interceptor is destroyed after it shoots down one drone
Minimum spacing You cannot place two interceptors within 5 blocks of each other
Detection radius Automatically shoots down drones within range

How it looks: When an interceptor successfully shoots down a drone you'll see a firework burst + electric spark effect at the interception point, and a server-wide message announces the intercept.

Commands Reference

Command Permission Description
/dronetarget <x> <y> <z> All players Launch held drone at coordinates
/dronecraftgui All players Open the crafting recipe GUI
/droneget <1|2|3|4> Admin only Give yourself a drone by tier
/droneget interceptor Admin only Give yourself an interceptor
