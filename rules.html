Server Rules

To keep our SMP fair, fun, and welcoming, all players must follow these rules. Breaking them can lead to warnings, mutes, or bans.

🔹 General Rules

  • ❌ No hacking, cheating, or exploits.
  • ❌ One account per player — no alts or punishment evasion.
  • ❌ No NSFW, illegal, or offensive content.
  • ❌ No toxicity, threats, or harassment.
  • ⚠️ Staff decisions are final — appeal respectfully if needed.
  • ❌ No TP traps.
  • 👥 Max team size: 4 players.

⚔️ Combat Rules (PvP)

  • ❌ No naked killing (unarmed/unarmored players).
  • ❌ No combat logging or teleporting during fights.
  • ❌ No tipped arrows (slow fall, instant damage).
  • ❌ No tridents, elytras, or flying in PvP.
  • ❌ No spawn-killing or repeated targeting.
  • ✅ Totems are unlimited per fight.
  • ❌ No crystals, bedbombs, or TNT minecarts.
  • ❌ No anchors or CPvP.
  • 📦 Max 16 ender pearls per fight.

🏚️ Raiding & Exploits

  • ⚔️ Raiding & griefing are allowed.
  • ❌ No zombie villager claiming unless you cured it.
  • ❌ No nether/end bases — farms only.
  • ❌ No dupers, lag machines, or exploits (including string dupers).
  • 🎥 ReplayMod allowed only for reports.

🧪 Potions & Items

  • ❌ Slow falling (potions/arrows) is banned.
  • 🎒 Event/PvP items must stay in your inventory.
  • ❌ No storing event items in ender chests, team ender chests, or stashing them.
  • 🚫 If found stored, the items will be removed and reused in future events.

🔧 Mods & Clients

  • ✅ Allowed: Optifine, minimap (no entities), ReplayMod (for reports).
  • ❌ Banned: Freecam, X-ray, macros, auto-clickers, PvP assists.

🔊 Discord Rules

  • 📜 Follow Discord ToS & Guidelines.
  • ❌ No spam, mass pings, impersonation, or off-topic promo.
  • ❌ No advertising without staff permission.
  • 📂 Use channels correctly — memes in memes, serious in serious.

📝 Staff Application Rules

  • ❌ Asking a staff member to read your application will result in automatic rejection.
  • ⏳ There is a 3-day wait period for staff applications.

🚨 Enforcement

Punishments include Warnings, Temp bans, or Permanent bans.
❗ Lying to staff = stricter punishments. Be honest.

Rules are now active. Changes may happen to keep balance. For help, open a ticket or ping staff.
